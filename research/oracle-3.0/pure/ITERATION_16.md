## Why a NEW independent distribution is required

In iteration 15, the first complete mathematically independent data/training
pipeline PASSED, yet the native teacher found almost trivial decisions
because the independent natural samplers produced weak scorecards:
at three remaining turns mean already-scored Small Points were ~104
(uniform), ~184 (score-aware), ~172 (scratch), and no game had yet
crossed the four upper-category Big Point thresholds. The new neural
student selected on validation was slightly WORSE than the independent
purely myopic mathematical strategy on the 24-game untouched test.
This is a NEGATIVE result, not a reason to imitate production Oracle.

To test challenging optimal decisions, construct **lawfully reachable**
but **deliberately rare/high-reward** states around threshold bonuses,
zero scores, category interactions, Straight, Full House and Balut. We
must never pretend their relative prevalence reflects typical gameplay.
This experiment is an exact-math *coverage and decision-fidelity* test,
not population inference.

## Precommitted new experiment (before results)

- **120 new independent seed games** \`960200000..960200119\`.
- **40 each** stratified mathematical profiles: \`threshold\`, \`bonus\`,
  \`strike\`. Half-built scorecards never come from any old advisor.
- Each scorecard is generated by a full **28-step replayable, legal
  category/dice/column scoring witness**. In the first 25 turns, sample
  scores from explicitly specified, positive-probability five-die
  multisets: rare high outcomes, medium threshold-near outcomes,
  and controlled scratch/zero outcomes. The final three turns use actual
  independent seeded uniform dice and legal category-oriented holds.
  Any one of the selected first-roll outcomes is possible with a
  strictly positive probability under fair dice — though an entire
  high-scoring witness is often extremely rare. **Not normal-game
  distribution**.
- Choose missing-category patterns including multi-slot gaps,
  three-category competition, four/six/choice thresholds and nonlinear
  Straight/Full House/Balut bonuses.
- Four present-roll Bellman roots per game (3 observed endgame roots
  with 3/2/1 turns left + one deliberately possible alternate dice root).
  Three unique pre-first-roll V* labels per game.
- Full game-level split **72 train / 24 validation / 24 locked test**
  by consecutive seeds: \`960200000..071\`, \`072..095\`, \`096..119\`.
  Profiles balanced 24/8/8 per split. No prior iteration15 train/test
  scorecards or neural weights reused.
- Oracle 2.0 v46 immutable native Bellman returns numerical COMPLETE
  Float64 optimal Q/V for 1–3 remaining turns, cap **500k DP states**,
  timeout **20 s per query**. Failed cases explicit, never imputed.
- Independently audit every one of the 3,360 legal scoring witness steps,
  all native Q action keys and max values, dataset splits, actual
  distribution support for bonus/threshold states and zero alternatives.
- Train new independent mathematically optimal scorecard continuation V*
  on only 72 games, with math-only stage/linear/17→20→1 neural models.
  Model weights and training normalization are fresh, never from
  production or the older easy-scorecard study.
- Evaluate native Oracle 2.0 per-action optimality with an **independent
  multinomial within-turn expectimax** using each candidate. Select
  myopic/stage/linear/neural solely by validation exact Q regret.
  Lock that choice before test. Locked 24-game results: full exact-Q
  regret, optimal-action agreement, 95% seed-game bootstrap CI,
  error by 1/2/3 turns, scratch/bonus/threshold, natural versus
  deliberately targeted dice, value MAE/RMSE, latency, process RSS.
- A mathematically **exact numerical** terminal action is available
  from native Oracle 2.0; no fallback or untrained inference in any
  state with >3 turns left. No full-game final BP superiority claim.
