# ADR-025 — Iteration 30: ordinary 20–28-turn optimality still unresolved (negative result)
**Date: 2026-11-02. Status: documented negative research, no production change.**

## Target
Find a mathematically proven globally optimal present action on a NORMAL nontrivial position with 20–28 turns remaining, all legal score and reroll alternatives and all future category choices globally adaptive. User explicitly rejected the prior specially constructed five-sixes Balut-50 certificate as trivial. We have NOT achieved the target.

## New method and objective check
Generalized nonanticipative first-future-category policy swap to EVERY pair of scoring categories. For every one of 252 possible future ordered-dice histograms y, require exact integer nonnegative total Small Points difference and preservation of both relevant category reward components (or proof that a component is permanently fixed). For any future adaptive strategy following an alternative current score a, score candidate c now and score a upon the first future c occasion, following the virtual strategy throughout. This is a VALID GLOBAL WEAK DOMINANCE SUFFICIENT TEST, not a globally complete solver and not necessarily strict optimality.

## Locked, measured facts
- 252/252 unordered first dice outcomes on an EMPTY 28-turn card, two rerolls: **0 additional action eliminations** beyond V29, **0 globally optimal top-choice certificates**. Baseline V29's 1,097 eliminations (mainly dominated zero-scoring-now actions) unchanged.
- 36 actual observed early-game scorecards/dice from four full legal game traces at horizons 20,21,...,28: **0 additional eliminations**, **0 full top certifications**. Old V29 eliminations remain 145.
- Preexisting V28 mathematical lower/upper candidate comparison on ALL 252 opening rolls: **0 actions eliminated with current interval bounds, 0 certified best actions**. Best legal-policy lower versus largest rival admissible upper has mean deficit **16.6913539424 expected Big Points**, MINIMUM deficit **15.0643652163 BP** (dice 3-3-3-3-3; candidate Balut). These are proof gaps, **not measured true action regrets**. V28 bound evaluation uses double precision and is not outward-rounded numerical proof.
- Unchanged native V26 exact Bellman on empty 28-turn card with 3-3-3-3-3 and two rerolls hit explicit 50,000 W-state cap after about 0.66 seconds, returned `Exact state budget exceeded`, not an exact result. This cap failure is NOT a complexity impossibility proof.
- Positive but excluded from 'success' metrics: an intentionally contrived legal **20-turn r=0** card with Choice and Balut both completely filled, dice five fours, proves weak global best Score Fours20 over four scoring-zero alternatives. Separate Python scorer independently checked **31,104** integer comparisons over all 6^5 ordered future dice outcomes for four alternative categories. This is exactly the kind of obvious constructed max-value case the user does not want. Does NOT certify any reroll action, uniqueness or representative 28-turn play.

## Conclusion and next substantive research path
Do NOT label this as an optimal Oracle or milestone achieved. The earlier paths of more policy-model training, V28 policy lower optimization and V29/V30 pairwise swaps do not bridge the 15–17 BP admissible interval gap at the opening. A genuinely tighter **coupled-category global U(s,a)** is required: investigate finite-horizon stochastic information relaxation with admissible nonanticipativity penalties, Lagrangian resource prices for one-category-per-turn coupling, or exact factored/ADD symbolic Bellman. TEST validity on independent V27 exact Q roots and report *actual* action elimination and at least one nontrivial observed 20–28 optimality certificate before further promotion. Exact seven-turn endgame remains mandatory. If resource feasibility cannot be shown, report uncertainty honestly.
