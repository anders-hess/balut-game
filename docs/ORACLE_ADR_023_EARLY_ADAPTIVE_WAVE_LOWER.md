# ADR-023 — Exact-expectation legal seven-category wave-policy lower bounds for 20–28 turns
**Date:** 2026-11-02. **Status:** ACCEPTED RESEARCH BOUND COMPONENT, NOT DEPLOYMENT / NOT GLOBAL OPTIMALITY.
**Binding:** North Star, ADR-007, ADR-010/011, ADR-019/020/021/022. Global objective is optimal expected FINAL Big Points for every dice/scorecard/remaining-roll decision across all 28 turns. All seven categories adaptive. Exactly solve <=7.

## The mathematical idea
Partition a scorecard's remaining category slots into at most four consecutive 'waves': wave j offers exactly one slot of category c if current quota `remaining_slots[c] > j`. Within each wave the legal controller chooses score category adaptively after observing the dice, may hold/rethrow with the normal two rerolls, and never reuses a category until the next wave. After its quota mask empties, proceed to the next wave. This is a strictly LEGAL, fully specified but generally SUBOPTIMAL policy.

Within a wave, compute the optimal dice/score choice for an explicit **fixed additive utility** by subset-mask Bellman DP on 127 nonempty subsets, with all 252 five-dice unordered histograms, 210 legal holds and 3 cast stages. At each mask, compute the complete probability distribution of the next actual `(scored category, Small Points)`. A second subset-mask DP integrates the entire wave to calculate BOTH category-specific Small Points marginal distributions AND the joint total Small Points sum distribution.

Crucial: wave policy is only a function of mask and current dice, not previously observed Small Points, so successive waves are independent under iid fair dice. Convolve per-category wave marginals to get threshold probability for 4/5/6 and Choice, all-future-success probabilities for Straight and Full House, and expected successful Baluts. Independently convolve each wave's **JOINT total Small Points sum** distribution to obtain exact `E[Small Points end-bonus]`, without incorrectly assuming category independence within a wave. Sum all expected final Big Point components to calculate an exact-in-real-arithmetic **policy-conditional lower bound**, no fitted prediction and no Monte Carlo. Optimize the *current* root choices over ALL legal scores and holds using exact dice chance kernels. Pointwise max over five fixed valid wave profiles and inherited V15 legal-policy lower remains valid.

Use legacy v15 independently verified admissible upper `U(s,a)` unchanged and report regret upper `max_a U(s,a) - max_a L(s,a)`; numerical computations are ordinary Float64 and therefore final outward-rounded machine certification still needs future work. Every early action remains `approvedAction=null` unless genuinely certified; V28 currently certified NONE.

## Exhaustive opening (28 remaining turns)
Enumerated all **252 distinct five-dice histograms** and weighted by fair-dice exact multinomial multiplicities. Inherited V15 best-root lower average **1.448264** -> new wave lower **4.843476** BP, **+3.395212 expected BP**. Average conservative global-regret bound **20.243946** -> **16.848734** BP, **−16.7715%**. 252/252 had a strictly better lower bound; 0/252 certified unique global top, zero safely excluded alternatives. 86 provisional lower-bound top actions changed (42.8% probability mass), BUT NONE of these changes is proven optimal and the old V15 upper bound is not tightened. Full 252 Node evaluation ~3.9 sec after baseline module import/precompute on Linux; NOT a device benchmark.

## Independent observed early scorecards
Generated 4 preselected seeded complete LEGAL 28-turn V6 games. At each actually encountered turn from 28 down through 20, recorded ACTUAL scorecard and first roll. 36 observations, 4 per horizon, no post-selection. Average global-root regret upper **16.978908 -> 14.140170 BP**, reduction **16.7192%**; all 9 horizons improved (~15.36–18.21% by horizon). 0 proved unique optimal early action; no proved action eliminations; 9 changed provisional lower-based choices. This is independent from the 252 empty-scorecard histogram enumeration, but not a representative population inference.

## Research decision and limitations
This is a real FIRST 20–28-turn algorithmic lower-bound improvement using an all-seven-category adaptive legal strategy and exact probability integration, not a trained score predictor. It is **NOT** a new optimal opening certification. The upper bound (~21+ BP on 28-turn openings) remains radically too loose; zero candidate actions were eliminated. The goal now MUST be a genuinely tighter coupled-category *upper* relaxation or dual proof-directed search, plus directed-rounding numerical enclosure as V27. Do not deploy until root action certification and realistic live latency are achieved.
